# Shared Borgi Visual Prompt

Create a polished high-fidelity visual for **Borgi**, a general-purpose booking and reminder SaaS product.

## Product identity

Borgi helps businesses and customers create bookings, manage schedules, send reminders, follow up, and reduce no-shows. The product must feel suitable for clinics, salons, consultants, education, events, meeting rooms, workshops, and service businesses. It must **not** look like a pet-care, veterinary, or grooming application.

## Canonical mascot identity

When reference images are attached, treat them as the canonical source of truth. Preserve the same mascot identity rather than redesigning or beautifying it.

Borgi is a short, round orange-and-cream corgi with:

- a slightly oversized head and very short rounded body
- soft toy-like 3D rendering
- rosy cheeks
- asymmetric goofy eyes
- a lovable blank or derpy expression
- an occasional tiny tongue-out smile
- the same orange-and-cream coat pattern
- a crooked oversized teal-and-orange propeller beanie
- an off-center mini propeller and uneven floppy hat sides
- a teal scarf
- a round gold bell tag

The hat is Borgi's signature element and must remain recognizable in every pose. Do not change the breed, face, proportions, coat pattern, hat, scarf, or bell.

## Product visual language

- Modern friendly SaaS, premium but approachable
- Light background and generous whitespace
- Rounded cards and subtle shadows
- Brand colors: teal `#21B6C1`, orange `#FF9A3C`, cream `#FFF3E0`, lilac `#8B6CFF`, sky `#E8F6FA`, dark brown `#5A3E2B`
- Rounded outline icons for calendar, clock, bell, customer, checklist, message, chart, branch, payment, and settings
- Clean data hierarchy and realistic product UX
- Soft upper-left studio lighting for 3D mascot artwork
- High consistency across all generated pages

## Screen composition rules

Create one coherent production-ready screen or asset, not a collage, mood board, presentation board, poster, or collection of unrelated screens unless the page prompt explicitly requests a design sheet.

For product screens:

- use a clear responsive grid
- maintain aligned columns and consistent spacing
- show realistic navigation and controls
- use restrained mascot placement
- keep Borgi secondary to the product task, generally no more than 15–25% of the screen
- avoid excessive decorative icons
- avoid fake brand marks and unrelated logos
- prefer short legible labels over large amounts of generated body text

## Avoid

- Pet-care visual language
- Paw-print backgrounds, bones, kennels, food bowls, veterinary tools, or grooming imagery
- Changing Borgi's breed, coat pattern, face, proportions, hat, scarf, or bell
- Hyper-realistic fur
- Perfectly symmetrical, heroic, aggressive, or overly intelligent facial expressions
- Excessive mascot placement that blocks UI
- Random decorative elements
- Garbled text, malformed controls, duplicated limbs, or floating UI without structure
- Recreating the exact layout of an attached style reference

## Output quality

Produce a professional concept suitable for a Figma design reference and implementation planning. Use aligned grids, consistent spacing, realistic components, intentional hierarchy, and sufficient whitespace. Prioritize visual coherence and mascot consistency over unnecessary decorative detail.
