# Shared Borgi Visual Prompt

Create a polished high-fidelity visual for **Borgi**, a general-purpose booking and reminder SaaS product.

## Product identity

Borgi helps businesses and customers create bookings, manage schedules, send reminders, follow up, and reduce no-shows. The product must feel suitable for clinics, salons, consultants, education, events, meeting rooms, workshops, and service businesses. It must **not** look like a pet-care, veterinary, or grooming application.

## Mascot identity

Use the same original mascot in every visual: a short, round orange-and-cream corgi with a slightly oversized head, soft toy-like 3D rendering, rosy cheeks, asymmetric goofy eyes, a lovable blank/derpy expression, and an occasional tiny tongue-out smile. Borgi wears a crooked oversized teal-and-orange propeller beanie with an off-center mini propeller and uneven floppy sides, a teal scarf, and a round gold bell tag. The hat is a signature element and must remain consistent.

## Visual language

- Modern friendly SaaS, premium but approachable
- Light background and generous whitespace
- Rounded cards and subtle shadows
- Brand colors: teal `#21B6C1`, orange `#FF9A3C`, cream `#FFF3E0`, lilac `#8B6CFF`, sky `#E8F6FA`, dark brown `#5A3E2B`
- Rounded outline icons for calendar, clock, bell, customer, checklist, message, chart, branch, payment, and settings
- Clean data hierarchy and realistic product UX
- Soft upper-left studio lighting for 3D mascot artwork
- High consistency across all generated pages

## Avoid

- Pet-care visual language
- Paw-print backgrounds, bones, kennels, food bowls, veterinary tools, or grooming imagery
- Changing the mascot's breed, coat pattern, hat, scarf, or bell
- Hyper-realistic fur
- Perfectly symmetrical or heroic facial expressions
- Excessive decorative mascot placement that blocks UI
- Garbled text, malformed controls, duplicated limbs, or floating UI without structure

## Output quality

Produce a professional concept suitable for a Figma design reference. Use legible interface labels, aligned grids, consistent spacing, realistic components, and enough whitespace for implementation.
