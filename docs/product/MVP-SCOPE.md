# Borgi MVP Scope

## Goal
Deliver a multi-business booking and reminder SaaS that reduces scheduling work and no-shows without looking like a pet-care product.

## Primary roles
- Owner: workspace, billing, branches, policies, integrations.
- Admin: day-to-day operations and reports.
- Staff: availability, assigned appointments, customer notes.
- Customer: book, reschedule, cancel, pay and receive reminders.

## In scope
1. Authentication and workspace onboarding.
2. Business profile, timezone, locale and booking policies.
3. Services, durations, buffers, prices and capacity.
4. Staff, branches, rooms and resource assignment.
5. Public booking page and embeddable booking link.
6. Availability calculation and conflict prevention.
7. Appointment create, confirm, reschedule, cancel and no-show.
8. Customer directory, history, notes and consent.
9. Email/LINE/SMS-ready notification abstraction and reminder rules.
10. Dashboard, today view, calendar, reports and CSV export.
11. Subscription, plan limits and audit log.

## Out of scope for MVP
Marketplace discovery, payroll, inventory, medical records, telemedicine, loyalty points, advanced campaign automation and native mobile apps.

## Success metrics
- Booking completion rate.
- Time to create a booking.
- Reschedule/cancel self-service rate.
- Reminder delivery rate.
- No-show rate.
- Weekly active workspaces.

## Definition of done
Every feature includes permission rules, responsive behavior, loading/empty/error states, analytics events, accessibility acceptance criteria and API ownership.
