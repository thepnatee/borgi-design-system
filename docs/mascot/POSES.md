# Borgi Pose Library

Approved reusable poses: front idle, three-quarter idle, wave, point, thinking, typing, holding calendar, holding reminder bell, holding payment card, celebrating, sleeping, searching, waiting and gentle error support.

Every pose must preserve the canonical silhouette, visible teal scarf, gold bell tag and crooked teal-and-orange propeller beanie unless the asset is explicitly marked `accessory-free`. Keep limbs short, gestures readable and props secondary. Avoid athletic realism, human anatomy, four-legged pet poses and poses implying veterinary or grooming use.
