# Borgi Accessories

## Signature accessories

- Crooked oversized teal-and-orange propeller beanie
- Teal scarf
- Round gold bell tag

These three elements define Borgi and should remain consistent in hero, marketing and system illustrations.

## Optional props

Calendar, clock, reminder bell, clipboard, tablet, payment card, chart, message bubble, ticket, branch sign and celebration confetti.

## Rules

Props must support booking and business workflows, remain visually secondary and use the Borgi palette. Do not add medical pet collars, leashes, grooming tools, bones, kennels or veterinary equipment. Never replace the signature hat with branded third-party headwear.
