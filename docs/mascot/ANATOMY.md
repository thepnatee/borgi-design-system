# Borgi Mascot Anatomy

## Canonical silhouette

Borgi is a short, round orange-and-cream corgi with a slightly oversized head and compact body. The silhouette must remain readable at 48 px and recognizable without accessories.

## Required proportions

- Head: approximately 45% of total height
- Body: compact oval with short legs
- Ears: large triangular ears with soft floppy tips
- Muzzle: short cream muzzle, never long or realistic
- Eyes: intentionally asymmetric and slightly goofy
- Tail: small and secondary to the silhouette

## Required markings

Cream muzzle, chest and inner-ear accents. Orange dominates the head and body. Rosy cheeks are optional at very small sizes.

## Rendering

Use soft toy-like 3D materials, rounded forms, gentle shadows and clean edges. Avoid realistic fur, sharp anatomy, aggressive canine posture or pet-care visual cues.
