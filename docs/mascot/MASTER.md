# Borgi Mascot Master Specification

## Canonical identity

Borgi is a short, round orange-and-cream corgi with a slightly oversized head, soft toy-like 3D rendering, rosy cheeks, asymmetric goofy eyes, a lovable blank expression, and an occasional tiny tongue-out smile.

Borgi always wears:

- A crooked oversized teal-and-orange propeller beanie
- An off-center mini propeller
- Uneven floppy sides on the hat
- A teal scarf
- A round gold bell tag

These elements are identity-critical and must remain consistent.

## Shape language

- Large rounded head
- Compact rounded torso
- Short legs
- Soft triangular ears with slightly floppy tips
- Small rounded paws
- Low visual center of gravity
- No sharp or aggressive anatomy

## Coat pattern

- Warm orange main coat
- Cream muzzle, chest, inner ears, and lower face
- Clear but soft transitions between orange and cream
- No realistic fur strands
- No alternate breed markings

## Face

- Eyes are intentionally asymmetric
- Expression is gentle, curious, and slightly derpy
- Nose is small and dark brown
- Mouth is minimal
- Tongue may appear only as a small accent
- Avoid heroic, angry, smug, or perfectly symmetrical expressions

## Rendering

- Soft toy-like 3D
- Matte materials with subtle highlights
- Soft upper-left studio lighting
- Gentle contact shadow
- Clean silhouette
- Premium SaaS illustration quality

## Scale in product UI

- Hero illustration: 25–40% of composition
- Empty state: 20–30%
- Inline helper: 8–15%
- Notification or badge: 5–10%

Borgi must not obscure primary actions, data, labels, or form fields.

## Approved pose families

- Neutral front
- Three-quarter standing
- Waving
- Pointing or presenting
- Thinking
- Checking a calendar
- Holding a reminder bell
- Celebrating
- Waiting patiently
- Gentle concern for recoverable errors

## Prohibited changes

- Removing or replacing the propeller beanie
- Changing scarf color
- Removing the bell tag
- Changing breed
- Hyper-realistic fur
- Human-like hands
- Long limbs
- Aggressive teeth or eyebrows
- Pet-care props
- Excessive costume changes
