# Borgi Do and Don't

## Do

- Preserve the asymmetric eyes, compact body and toy-like rendering.
- Keep the teal scarf, gold bell and crooked propeller beanie consistent.
- Use Borgi as a helpful product companion, not the subject of every screen.
- Maintain clear whitespace and readable silhouette.
- Match expression intensity to the product state.

## Don't

- Do not make Borgi realistic, aggressive, skinny, tall or anatomically human.
- Do not turn the product into a pet-care, veterinary or grooming brand.
- Do not change the signature colors or mirror markings inconsistently.
- Do not use excessive accessories, text embedded in artwork or busy backgrounds.
- Do not use Borgi to trivialize payment failures, privacy problems or destructive actions.
