# Staff Management Flow

Managers invite staff by email, assign one or more roles, branches, services and working hours, then send a time-limited invitation. Acceptance requires authenticated identity and tenant confirmation.

Permission changes take effect immediately for new requests and create audit events. Removing staff disables future access, unassigns future availability according to policy and never deletes historical appointment ownership. Conflicts, expired invitations, duplicate users and last-owner removal must be handled explicitly.
