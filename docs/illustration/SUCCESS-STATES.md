# Success-State Illustrations

Approved scenarios: booking confirmed, payment completed, reminder scheduled, customer added, staff invited, settings saved, report exported and integration connected.

Use a joyful but compact Borgi pose, one workflow-specific prop and restrained confetti. Keep the action result visually dominant and avoid implying that irreversible actions are always safe.

Success copy must identify what completed, include the useful next step and expose reference numbers or dates as selectable text outside the artwork.
