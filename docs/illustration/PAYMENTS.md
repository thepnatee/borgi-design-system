# Payment Illustrations

Payment scenes cover awaiting payment, paid, failed, refunded, partially refunded and receipt ready. Borgi may hold a neutral payment card, receipt or coin-like token without imitating a real financial brand.

Amounts, currencies, fees, refund timing and transaction references must remain live text. Do not display card numbers, bank credentials or invented compliance badges in artwork. Failed-payment art must remain supportive and avoid celebration cues.
