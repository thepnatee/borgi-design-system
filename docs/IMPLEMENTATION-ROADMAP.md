# Borgi Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Brand foundation

- Approve mascot master sheet and turnaround
- Finalize logo variants and app icon
- Confirm color, typography, icon, illustration, and voice rules
- Export reference mascot images with transparent backgrounds

## Phase 2 — Product foundations

- Build tokens and component library
- Establish desktop, tablet, and mobile grids
- Implement accessibility and interaction-state standards
- Create Storybook or equivalent documentation

## Phase 3 — Customer MVP

- Marketing landing
- Authentication
- Business onboarding
- Public booking landing
- Service, staff, date, and time selection
- Customer information and consent
- Summary, payment, success, manage, reschedule, and cancel

## Phase 4 — Admin MVP

- Dashboard
- Today
- Calendar
- Appointment create/detail/list
- Customers
- Services
- Staff
- Branches
- Reminder templates and delivery log
- General booking settings

## Phase 5 — Business expansion

- Resources and capacity
- Customer groups
- Feedback
- Advanced reports
- Roles and permissions
- Billing and usage
- Integrations, API, and webhooks
- Audit and security

## Asset production order

1. CI-01 mascot master
2. CI-02 logo system
3. CI-03 design system
4. MKT-01 landing
5. BOOK-01 through BOOK-03
6. ADM-01 through ADM-09
7. SYS-01 and SYS-02

Generate low-quality drafts first. Approve composition and character consistency before medium/high-quality regeneration.
