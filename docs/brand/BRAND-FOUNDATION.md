# Borgi Brand Foundation

## Purpose

Borgi helps service businesses make booking, scheduling, reminders, follow-up, and customer communication simple and reliable.

## Vision

Make every appointment feel easy, clear, and cared for.

## Mission

Provide an approachable booking platform that reduces manual work, prevents no-shows, and helps businesses build better customer relationships.

## Positioning

Borgi is a general-purpose booking and reminder SaaS product for clinics, salons, consultants, education providers, events, workshops, meeting rooms, and other service businesses.

Borgi is not a pet-care, veterinary, grooming, or game product.

## Core values

1. **Clarity** — users should always understand what happened and what to do next.
2. **Reliability** — bookings, reminders, and schedules must feel dependable.
3. **Warmth** — communication should feel human without becoming childish.
4. **Efficiency** — common work should require fewer steps.
5. **Inclusivity** — language, interaction, and visuals should work for diverse users.

## Brand personality

- Friendly
- Calm
- Helpful
- Professional
- Playful in moderation
- Trustworthy

## Brand promise

> Borgi keeps every booking on track.

## Brand keywords

`friendly SaaS`, `booking assistant`, `clear`, `organized`, `warm`, `reliable`, `lightweight`, `approachable`

## Audience

### Primary

- Small and medium service businesses
- Operations teams
- Reception and front-desk staff
- Independent professionals
- Event and workshop organizers

### Secondary

- Customers creating or managing bookings
- Managers reviewing performance
- Staff receiving schedules and reminders

## Do

- Show real booking and operational workflows.
- Use clear hierarchy and realistic interface states.
- Keep Borgi supportive rather than dominant.
- Use whitespace, rounded components, and calm visual rhythm.
- Explain errors with a recovery action.

## Do not

- Use pet-care language or pet-industry imagery.
- Add paw prints, bones, kennels, food bowls, grooming tools, or veterinary symbols.
- Make the product feel like a toy or children's game.
- Use Borgi as decoration when it blocks useful information.
- Use jokes in critical errors, payments, privacy, or destructive actions.
