# Borgi Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand idea

**Borgi remembers the booking so people can focus on the moment.**

Borgi is a booking, reminder, follow-up, and customer-care platform for general service businesses. The brand combines operational reliability with a slightly absurd, lovable mascot.

### Positioning

- Friendly enough for first-time business owners
- Reliable enough for multi-branch operations
- Flexible across clinics, salons, consultants, education, events, rooms, workshops, and service teams
- Never visually positioned as a pet, veterinary, or grooming product

### Tagline options

- Booking made simple. Borgi remembers the rest.
- Your goofy booking buddy.
- Book it. Remind it. Borgi it.
- Every appointment, remembered.

## 2. Brand personality

- Helpful
- Reliable
- Playful
- Slightly derpy
- Calm under pressure
- Never childish or chaotic

The humor comes from Borgi's expression and timing, not from weakening the product's credibility.

## 3. Mascot construction

Borgi is a short, round orange-and-cream corgi with:

- Oversized soft head and tiny body
- Orange coat and warm cream muzzle, chest, belly, paws, and forehead stripe
- Asymmetric goofy eyes, usually looking in slightly different directions
- Rosy circular cheeks
- Tiny visible tongue
- Small dark-brown nose
- Floppy ear tips rather than perfectly upright ears
- Crooked teal-and-orange propeller beanie with an off-center propeller
- Teal scarf tied loosely
- Round gold bell tag
- Soft toy-like 3D fur and rounded forms

### Expression rules

Borgi should look lovable and slightly confused. Avoid polished heroic expressions, sharp anime eyes, aggressive smiles, or perfect symmetry.

### Signature hat

The propeller beanie is mandatory in primary brand artwork. It should look handmade, slightly oversized, and a little crooked. The propeller may indicate system state:

- Slow spin: waiting or processing
- Fast spin: new booking or celebration
- Bent propeller: error or maintenance
- Stopped propeller: empty state or offline

### Mascot do

- Keep the same coat pattern, hat, scarf, and bell
- Keep proportions consistent
- Use Borgi as a guide, helper, narrator, or visual accent
- Let Borgi interact with calendar, bell, clock, phone, checklist, chart, map, or laptop

### Mascot don't

- Do not add bones, kennels, paw-print wallpaper, food bowls, grooming tools, or veterinary props as core UI imagery
- Do not turn every icon into a dog or paw
- Do not change breed, fur pattern, hat silhouette, scarf color, or bell shape
- Do not make Borgi too competent-looking or too baby-like

## 4. Logo system

Required logo variants:

- Primary horizontal wordmark
- Wordmark with tagline
- Icon-only B or Borgi head
- Monochrome dark
- Monochrome white
- Small-size favicon
- App icon

The letter O may contain a subtle paw or booking-check symbol, but the overall logo must still read as a SaaS brand rather than a pet service.

Minimum clear space: at least the height of the letter O around all sides.

## 5. Color system

### Brand

- Borgi Teal: `#21B6C1`
- Borgi Orange: `#FF9A3C`
- Borgi Cream: `#FFF3E0`
- Borgi Lilac: `#8B6CFF`
- Sky Tint: `#E8F6FA`
- Dark Brown: `#5A3E2B`

### Semantic

- Success: `#20B486`
- Warning: `#F5A524`
- Error: `#F04438`
- Info: `#3B82F6`

### Neutral

- Ink: `#20242C`
- Secondary text: `#667085`
- Border: `#E4E7EC`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Canvas: `#F7F9FC`

Use teal for trust and primary action, orange for attention and Borgi accents, lilac for secondary navigation and scheduling states.

## 6. Typography

Recommended Thai and Latin pairing:

- Product UI: LINE Seed Sans TH
- Fallback: Noto Sans Thai, Inter, system sans-serif
- Marketing display: LINE Seed Sans TH Bold or Manrope Bold

Avoid overly cartoonish display fonts inside the product.

## 7. Iconography

- Rounded outline icons
- 1.75–2 px stroke at 24 px
- Sizes: 16, 20, 24, 32
- Filled icons only for selected states and strong alerts
- Core metaphors: calendar, clock, bell, person, branch, service, checklist, message, chart, payment

## 8. Illustration style

- Soft premium 3D
- Warm studio lighting from upper-left
- Gentle ambient shadow
- Rounded geometry
- Generous whitespace
- Light neutral background
- No photorealistic fur
- No busy pet-themed environments

## 9. Voice and tone

### Default

Clear, friendly, and useful.

> Booking confirmed. Borgi will help remember the rest.

### Reminder

> Your appointment is coming up in 1 hour.

### Empty state

> No bookings yet. Your calendar is ready when you are.

### Error

> My propeller spun the wrong way. Please try again.

### 404

> Looks like we arrived at the wrong appointment.

Use humor as a small supporting layer. Never hide critical information behind jokes.
