# AI Output Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before accepting any generated Borgi asset.

## Brand consistency
- Borgi remains an orange-and-cream corgi with asymmetric goofy eyes.
- Crooked teal-and-orange propeller beanie, teal scarf and gold bell remain present.
- Product reads as booking SaaS, not pet-care.
- Brand colors are used as accents rather than flooding the whole screen.

## Product realism
- Layout has a clear hierarchy and real application structure.
- Navigation, forms, tables and cards are aligned and plausible.
- Labels are legible and not duplicated or garbled.
- Empty, loading, success and failure states are purposeful.

## Composition
- Mascot supports the message and does not block controls.
- Spacing is consistent.
- Perspective and lighting are coherent.
- No duplicated limbs, floating props or malformed UI.

## Acceptance
Reject any asset that fails mascot identity, product category, readability or structural realism. Record accepted assets in `assets/index.json` with source prompt, model, date and intended usage.
