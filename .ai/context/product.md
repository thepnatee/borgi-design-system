# Borgi Product Context

## Positioning

Borgi is a practical booking and reminder product for Thai service SMEs. It should help a shop start quickly, manage daily appointments and reduce no-shows without requiring technical staff.

## Initial customer profile

Small service businesses with approximately one to five branches that currently coordinate bookings through LINE, phone calls, paper, spreadsheets or general calendar tools.

## Core value

- Accept bookings with less back-and-forth
- Keep staff schedules accurate
- Remind customers through familiar channels
- Handle cancellations and rescheduling clearly
- Record payment status without forcing a gateway
- Give owners a simple view of today's operation

## MVP outcome

The business can configure services and staff, publish a booking journey, operate appointments every day, contact customers, record manual payments and understand today's bookings.

## Evidence before expansion

New scope should be driven by interviews, observed usage, support requests, retention or willingness to pay. Roadmap status alone is not evidence.
